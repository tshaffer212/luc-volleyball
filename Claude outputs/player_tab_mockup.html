Layout

One scrolling page per player with a sticky section bar (Overview · Attacking · Passing · Serving · Block & Defense · Trends). Sections a player doesn't play (a libero's Attacking) hide automatically. Player list on the left stays as today.

Filters (top chips)

The same filters as Reports, applied to this one player: season, Matches / Practices / Both, pick specific sessions, Set, Pass scale, and Compare to (his position average, team average, or another player side by side).

At a glance

Six tiles chosen by position. Outsides: attack eff, eff-over-expected, pass avg, FBSO on his passes, ace/error, pass & hit. Middles swap passing for blocking + quick eff; liberos get pass avg, perfect %, zone, dig; setters get setter EOE / adjusted eff and set distribution. Each tile shows the comparison (▲/▼ vs position avg).

Where his points come from

The Points Added breakdown by skill (Basic / Situation-adjusted toggle) plus his rank in his position.

Auto insights

3–4 sentences generated from the numbers: biggest strength, biggest gap vs position, any large split (pass & hit, a weak set type or rotation). Only shown when the sample is big enough.

Attacking

Totals (K | E | BLK | Att | Eff | K%), by set type, by setter, by situation vs the team on the same balls, hitter EOE/Adjusted, by rotation (R1–R6 grid, setter zone shown), Pass & Hit.

Passing

Grade mix (count + %), float vs topspin, zones 1/6/5 on a court, and what happened after his pass (FBSO %, sideout %, Points Added from passing).

Serving / Block / Defense

Full counts, serve type, point-scoring % on his serve, block stuff/touch/err, dig good/err and dig→kill %.

Setters (when the player is a setter)

Adds a Setting section: setter EOE / Standardized / Adjusted, set distribution by situation, efficiency by hitter he sets, and his set-choice credit from Points Added.
